Before we wrap up, let me answer the question I get most often. People ask me which camera they should buy. My honest answer is that your phone is already good enough. The thing that actually ruins most videos is bad sound. If people cannot hear you clearly, they will scroll away in less than a second, no matter how beautiful the picture looks. So if you have a small budget, spend it on a simple microphone first, and only later think about a camera.

The second question is about growth. Many creators feel stuck at a few hundred followers and think something is wrong with the algorithm. Usually nothing is wrong. They are simply posting what they want to say instead of what their audience wants to hear. Read your comments. Look at the questions people ask again and again. Each repeated question is a video idea that already has demand. When I started doing this, my views doubled in just six weeks.

And finally, protect your energy. Creating content every day is a marathon, not a sprint. Batch your work. Record on one day, edit on another day, and schedule your posts in advance. When you stop treating every single post like an exam, you start enjoying the process again, and your audience can feel that difference.
